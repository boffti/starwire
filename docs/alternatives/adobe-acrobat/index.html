Open source alternatives to Adobe Acrobat

4 projects on starwire do the same job. Ranked by how much of what Adobe Acrobat does each one covers, read from its README and website. Ties go to the most active.

What Adobe Acrobat does

  1. View and annotate PDFscore
  2. Edit text and images in PDFscore
  3. Convert PDFs to and from Office filescore
  4. Fill and sign forms
  5. Merge, split and organize pages
  6. OCR for scanned documents
  7. Compress and protect PDFs
  8. Request e-signatures

Each dot in the table is one job, in this order. Square dots are core jobs and count double. Full means yes, half means partly. "See the evidence" under a project shows the line behind each one.
